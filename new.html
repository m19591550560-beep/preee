01 Research Content

What does the core paper study?

研究内容

The study examines whether the digital economy promotes agricultural new quality productive forces, and further explores the mechanisms and regional differences behind this relationship.

To address the limited empirical evidence in previous studies, the authors construct comprehensive evaluation index systems and use Chinese provincial panel data for empirical testing.

Whether?Does the digital economy significantly promote agricultural new quality productive forces?
How?Does digital literacy act as an important transmission mechanism?
When & where?Do regional conditions, economic development and urbanization change the effect?
中文辅助

文章关注数字经济能否促进农业新质生产力，并进一步分析作用机制与区域差异。

作者利用中国省级面板数据进行实证检验，同时考察人才数字素养的中介作用，以及经济发展和城镇化水平对赋能效果的影响。

02 Theoretical Framework

How does the digital economy empower agriculture?

理论基础

The paper builds a framework combining direct empowerment and an indirect mechanism.

Digital InfrastructureData collection, connectivity and intelligent decision-making.
Digital IndustrializationTechnology and digital-service development.
Industrial DigitalizationDigital transformation of agricultural production and circulation.
Innovation PotentialTechnology application, innovation and value-chain upgrading.
Indirect channel: Digital economy → stronger digital literacy → better use of digital tools, more informed decisions and stronger innovation capacity → higher agricultural new quality productive forces.
中文辅助

直接作用包括数字基础设施、数字产业化、产业数字化和数字创新潜力。

间接作用重点是人才数字素养：数字经济通过提升从业者运用数字工具、科学决策和技术创新的能力，进一步促进农业新质生产力。

03 Research Design

What data and empirical methods are used?

研究设计

The study uses provincial panel data from 31 Chinese regions covering 2012–2022.

Two Composite IndicesDigital economy + agricultural new quality productive forces.
Entropy Weight MethodIndicator weights are determined by variation in the data.
Two-way Fixed EffectsControls for province-specific differences and common year effects.

The study also applies mediation analysis, robustness tests, endogeneity tests, regional heterogeneity analysis and threshold regression.

中文辅助

样本为2012—2022年中国31个省级地区，数据主要来自《中国统计年鉴》《中国农村统计年鉴》等。

农业新质生产力从农业劳动者、农业劳动对象和农业劳动生产资料三个维度衡量。

方法包括熵权法、双向固定效应、中介效应、稳健性和内生性检验、区域异质性及门槛回归。

06 / 10
05 Research Conclusions

What is the main takeaway?

研究结论

The digital economy is an important driver of agricultural new quality productive forces, but its effects are not uniform across regions or development conditions.

InfrastructureStrengthen rural digital infrastructure.
Human CapitalImprove agricultural workers’ digital literacy.
Regional PolicyAdopt differentiated policies based on local conditions.
Data GovernanceImprove agricultural data management and institutional support.
中文辅助

数字经济的重要作用不仅体现在技术升级，也体现在数字素养提升、生产要素优化配置和农业产业链数字化转型。

由于存在区域差异及发展阶段差异，统一的发展模式并不适合所有地区。

08 Why This Core Paper?

Why do we still choose this paper as the core reference?

为什么最终选择这篇作为核心文献？
1
Almost perfect topic match

It directly links the digital economy with agricultural new quality productive forces, which closely matches our research title.

2
More complete theoretical structure

It integrates digital infrastructure, digital industrialization, industrial digitalization and digital innovation potential with agricultural workers, objects of labor and means of production.

3
Mechanism + empirical evidence

It does not stop at a conceptual discussion: it tests direct effects, digital-literacy mediation, robustness, endogeneity, regional heterogeneity and dimensional effects.

4
Strong practical extension

Its conclusions can naturally support practical pathways involving digital infrastructure, human capital, technology application, regional coordination and data governance.

中文辅助

一是题目匹配度最高：同时直接覆盖“数字经济”和“农业新质生产力”。

二是理论框架更完整：既解释数字经济的多个维度，也对应农业新质生产力的劳动者、劳动对象和生产资料。

三是实证链条更完整：不仅验证是否有效，还解释为什么有效、在哪里更有效、什么条件下更有效。

四是更容易转化为实践路径：能够延伸到数字基础设施、数字素养、技术应用、区域协调和数据治理。
